# Real photograph test sources

Retrieved from the scikit-image v0.25.2 repository:

- Astronaut: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/v0.25.2/skimage/data/astronaut.png` — NASA photograph of Eileen Collins, public domain, according to the scikit-image documentation.
- Chelsea: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/v0.25.2/skimage/data/chelsea.png` — Stefan van der Walt, CC0.
- Coffee: `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/scikit-image/scikit-image/v0.25.2/skimage/data/coffee.png` — Rachel Michetti, CC0; courtesy of Pikolo Espresso Bar.

Documentation: https://scikit-image.org/docs/stable/api/skimage.data

Original files are in `tmp/advanced/source`. Benchmark images were proportionally resized, when needed, to a maximum of 512 pixels on the longest side. These are conversion tests, not machine learning training data.

All other cases in this benchmark were generated by the project scripts.
